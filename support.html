Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us

Contact

Email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown in Settings › About). We usually reply within a few days.

Purchases

I bought something but didn't get it

Open Settings › Restore purchases. Some payment methods take a while to confirm; once Google Play confirms the payment, the item is delivered the next time you open the game. If it still hasn't arrived after 24 hours, email us your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone

Remove Ads and an active Tide Club subscription come back with Settings › Restore purchases, as long as you use the same Google account. Coins and progress are stored on the phone, so they only carry over through Android's own backup.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Google Play, so refunds are requested from Google: see Google Play refund help. You can also email us and we'll help.

Tide Club subscription

How do I cancel?

Open the Google Play Store › your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Tidepool › Cancel subscription. You keep the benefits until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Will I be charged after the free trial?

Yes, unless you cancel before the trial ends. The price and trial length are shown next to the subscribe button and in Google Play's confirmation screen.

Ads and privacy

How do I change my ad consent choices?

Open Settings › Privacy options in the app. This option appears in regions where consent is required (for example the EU, EEA and UK).

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the shop to remove all interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded videos stay available but are always optional.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game stores is on your phone: Settings › Reset all progress clears your game, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data. We don't run servers or accounts, so there's no copy with us. For data Google holds for ads or purchases, see the privacy policy.

Notifications

How do I stop reminders?

Turn off Settings › Notifications in the game, or block notifications for Tidepool in Android's settings. Reminders never arrive at night.
